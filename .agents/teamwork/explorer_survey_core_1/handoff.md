## 2. Logic Chain

1. **Step 11 Requirement**: Step 11 of `instructivo.md` and `BUYACAR_FINAL_INTEGRATED.md` requires:
   "En `AUTOPILOT`, revalidar disponibilidad inmediatamente antes del envío, verificar `sent_verified` o `send_failed`, comprobar idempotencia y guardar el resultado del CRM."
2. **Direct Comparison with Code**:
   - Observation 2 demonstrates that between Guard approval (CP5) and Channel reply (CP6), no revalidation takes place.
   - Observation 3 demonstrates that `sendRes.Status` is ignored when `err == nil`, allowing `send_failed` to pass as successful.
   - Observation 4 demonstrates that CRM task recording does not exist; tasks generated by AI or standard quote tasks from Fast-Path are discarded.
   - Observation 5 demonstrates that `IdempotencyRecord` lacks the audit metadata required to seal the transaction.
3. **Deduction of Gaps for R1**:
   - A pre-send revalidation method must be inserted between Guard validation and Channel reply.
   - Channel reply status must be evaluated against the operational mode (`SendSimulated` for SIMULATION, `SendDrafted` for DRAFT, `SendVerified` for AUTOPILOT).
   - An `internal/crm` package must be created to model and store CRM tasks thread-safely.
   - `IdempotencyRecord` must be enriched with `SendStatus`, `ETag`, and `CRMTaskID`.

## 3. Caveats

- **Scope boundary**: This investigation was strictly read-only and focused on Core, Pipeline, Inventory, and CRM (R1). Channels (R2/R3) were inspected only to understand `Channel.Reply()`, `SendStatus`, and test stub behaviors.
- **Assumptions**: We assume the upcoming R1 implementation will maintain clean architecture principles, introducing `internal/crm` with in-memory storage for `SIMULATION`/`DRAFT`, adhering to zero external dependencies.
- **Sold vehicle notification**: Fast-Path handles sold vehicles by returning `SoldVehicleNotice` and setting `MustEscalate: true`. The pre-send revalidator must recognize that this notification is already an escalation alert, rather than an erroneous promise of availability.

## 4. Conclusion

The current codebase is in an excellent, robust state (100% tests passing, clean modular structure), making it ideal for implementing R1.
The technical gaps for R1 are concrete and well-bounded:
1. Implement `RevalidatePreSend()` in `internal/core` using `IsFresh()` and `GetVehicle()` on `inventory.Cache`.
2. Check `sendRes.Status` after `ch.Reply()`, escalating on `send_failed` or unverified dispatch.
3. Create `internal/crm` (`Task`, `TaskType`, `Manager`, `MemoryManager`) to record CRM tasks.
4. Seal the idempotency record in `internal/store` with `SendStatus`, `ETag`, and `CRMTaskID`.
5. Wire these into `ProcessConversation()` and verify that `golden64_test.go` and `draft_batch_exerciser_test.go` continue to pass.
